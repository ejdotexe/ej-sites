Our story

Sactown Tacos started as a weekend cart outside a Franklin Blvd tire shop in 2016. Rosa's recipes, Luis's trompo, and a line that kept getting longer. Nine years later we're still a family truck — same salsas, same tortillas pressed every morning, a lot more Sacramento in the line.
